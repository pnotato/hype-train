# Interactions

## Talking
- Who shared it matters more than the outlet → listener's change scales with trust in the speaker [1] ✓
- Hearing a claim again raises belief, even if it's labelled false → repeat exposure adds belief [2] ✓
- Behaviour spreads better from several contacts than from one → 2+ sources count more than 2× one source [3] ✓
- News reaches most people through local opinion leaders, who differ by topic → Leader blobs relay outlets [4]
- People cluster with similar people → neighbours tend to share a shape [5]
- Sources from your own side seem more credible → same-shape speakers trusted more [6] ✓
- New info crosses groups through a few weak ties → border blobs bridge shapes [7]

## Family
- Parents pass political views to children → children start near a parent's perception [8]
- Children trust familiar adults' testimony → parent's words move a child more [9]

## Emotion
- False news spreads faster, driven by fear and surprise → alarming retellings spread further [10]
- Anxiety raises belief in and sharing of claims of any accuracy → worried blobs believe and share more [11] ✓
- Relying on emotion raises belief in fake news (not real news) → emotional framing works on false claims [12] ✓

## Sharing
- Sharing ≠ believing. Truth barely affects sharing intent → share chance and belief are separate rolls [13]
- People knowingly share conspiracies for likes → high socialness + low credulity still shares [14] ✓
- A tiny minority shares most fake news → few high-socialness blobs [15]
- 15% most habitual sharers shared ~37% of misinformation → habit, not belief, drives top sharers [16] ✓
- Retellings drop detail, exaggerate, and bend to fit the teller's views (levelling, sharpening, assimilation) → retelling styles [17]

## Hype and fallout
- Corrections never fully undo misinformation → debunked blobs don't return to start [18] ✓
- Satisfaction = performance vs expectation → underdelivering on a claim drops perception [19] ✓
- Very unhappy and very happy customers talk most (U-shape) → extreme perception raises socialness [20] ✓
- Trust is easier to destroy than build. Contested; strongest for negative events → losses > gains [21] ✓
- Generic fake-news warnings also cut belief in true headlines → after debunks, true claims land worse [22] ✓
- Repeated false alarms reduce response → each broken promise weakens the next claim [23]
- Adding ".com" to a name lifted stock with no business change → buzzword claims lift interest fast [24] ✓
- Regulator fined firms for false AI claims ("AI washing") → real-world parallel [25] ✓
- Belief can make itself true (bank runs) → optional: panic changes the world [26]
- Hype cycle: inflated expectations, then disillusionment → framing only, not evidence [27]

# Persona splits

## Age
- Canada ~19.5% 65+; under-18 ~20%. Exact share needs StatCan table 17-10-0005-01 → age distribution [28] ✓
- 65+ shared ~7× more fake-news links than 18–29 → elders share more online [29]
- Cause: digital literacy, source memory, social goals. Not decline [30]
- Trust rises with age → elders trust more, not less [31] ✓
- Older adults rate untrustworthy faces as more trustworthy → elders miss warning cues [32] ✓
- Some studies find younger people more likely to share → keep age off credulity [33]

## Credulity
- Gullibility is its own trait, separate from trust → credulity as its own axis [34] ✓
- Falling for fake headlines tracks low analytic thinking, not partisanship [35]

## Socialness
- See [15], [16]: spreading is concentrated → most blobs mid or low

## Persuasiveness
- Trust in the sharer drives belief [1]; leaders exist per community and topic [4]

## Recluse
- Long-term loneliness predicts conspiracy views. Recluses aren't skeptics → credulity not lowered [36] ✓

## Family
- Canada 2021: 25% of children in census families live with a lone parent (19% of under-15s) → families can have 1 adult [37] ✓
- Personality ~40% heritable → roll tags per blob, not per family [38] ✓
- Beliefs pass down more than personality [8]

## Shape (culture)
- Trust radius varies: some trust mainly in-group, others widely → shapes differ in out-group trust [39] ✓
- Familism: trust centred on family → Capsule [40]
- In-group sources seem more credible [6] ✓

## Motive
- Altruism was the strongest predictor of sharing COVID fake news → "warn" sharers [41] ✓
- Social-engagement motive [14] ✓
