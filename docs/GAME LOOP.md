# Game Loop

At every iteration of the game, a(weighted) random amount of blobs will begin talking to another blob. After which, each blob will report it's own perception and interest in the company. The average will be collected and plotted for that day. 

Blobs can also not talk to anyone and speak out in social media during each round. Some blobs may also just idle as they do not care.

The blobs finish after 50 rounds. Every 10 rounds, create a news story that will change the perception of the blobs.
