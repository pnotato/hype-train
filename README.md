# Hype Train

Working name. Hype train is a population simulation game where you control the news room for your AI company. Your goal is to get people talking about your product, so what do you report?

The goal of the simulation is to show how public perception of certain companies can be distorted through misinformation and hype. It's easy to get people to talk about your company by making bold claims, but what happens when you can't live up to it?

# The Game

The simulation runs a batch of 100 small LLM models, each given a specific persona, age range, and other traits that make them representitive of a Canadian Population. These are the 'blobs'.

The user will control the new media of your company. Every few rounds, the user will be given choices for how they want to present their product.
